Trump Account App Problems and Fixes

By j6 · Updated October 28, 2026 · Facts from IRS and Treasury sources (listed below) · Not tax advice

Short answer: most claim errors come from a mismatch. The name, SSN or birth date you typed must match what the IRS and Social Security have on file, for you and your child. Fix it in your IRS online account, then send a new claim form. You need a photo ID and a live selfie to verify. Delays of a few days can be normal.

Stay safe. The real app is "Trump Accounts" on the Apple App Store or Google Play, or trumpaccount.com. Activation emails come only from no-reply@trumpaccounts.treasury.gov. No one will ask for your password or a one-time code.

Fixes by error message

ProblemWhat to do
Can't verify my identityHave a valid government photo ID and good light for the live selfie. Use your legal name, SSN or ITIN and birth date exactly as on your tax return. If verification fails as the responsible party, sign in to your IRS online account, correct your election details, and submit a new claim form.
My child is ineligible / can't be verifiedUsually the child's name, SSN or birth date doesn't match your tax return or Social Security records. In your IRS online account go to My Elections, then Unable to Process, read the notice, correct it, and resubmit.
Child already has an accountOnly one account per child. Another parent or guardian may have claimed it first. The help center can't share who (tax privacy law). Talk to the co-parent, or contact support if you think it was not authorized.
Status says ProcessingWait for the email from no-reply@trumpaccounts.treasury.gov. IRS processing usually takes 1–3 business days.
Child turns 18 this yearToo late: the account must be opened before the year the child turns 18. This can't be overridden.
No SSN yet (newborn or parent)The child needs an SSN; you need an SSN or ITIN. Submit the claim form once you have them.
Forgot to elect the $1,000Not too late. Submit a new claim form with the pilot program election.
Started but didn't finishYour claim progress is saved. Sign back in with the same username and password. (The paper-style Form 4547 screens are not saved if you close before identity verification.)
Deposit reversed or card declinedMake sure the bank account has enough money; you get an email if a deposit is reversed, and many reversals can unlink the bank. A debit card must allow online purchases.

Check your claim status

  1. Sign in to your IRS online account with ID.me.
  2. Go to Forms, then Trump Accounts.
  3. Read the Election status table. "Unable to process" links to a notice that says why (see CP318 guide).

App basics

  • Works on iOS 16 or newer, Android 7 or newer, and Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge.
  • Tablets may work but are not officially supported.
  • Only the responsible party can log in. There is no shared login yet.
  • One login can manage several children: use Add child in the account switcher.
  • Help line: 866-USA-4547 (866-872-4547), as listed on IRS.gov/trumpaccounts. Or use the support pages in the app.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't the Trump Account app verify my identity?

The most common cause is a mismatch between what you entered (legal name, SSN or ITIN, date of birth) and IRS records. Correct your election in your IRS online account and submit a new claim form. Verification needs a government photo ID and a live selfie.

Why does it say my child already has an account?

Each child can have only one Trump Account, and the first eligible adult to complete the claim becomes the responsible party. Another parent or guardian may have claimed it.

How long does the Trump Account claim take?

The official help center says IRS processing of the claim form takes 1–3 business days; you then get an email from no-reply@trumpaccounts.treasury.gov to finish claiming.

Is there a deadline to claim the Trump Account?

No. Unclaimed accounts stay open and invested and become the child's traditional IRA at 18.

Can both parents log in?

No. Only the responsible party can log in for now; read-only access for a trusted contact is planned.
