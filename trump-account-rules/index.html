Trump Account Rules (2026)

By j6 · Updated October 28, 2026 · Facts from IRS and Treasury sources (listed below) · Not tax advice

All the key Trump Account numbers in one place, with links to the IRS and the law.

Words used here, in plain English
  • Section 530A: the part of the federal tax code that created Trump Accounts.
  • Traditional IRA: a retirement account. You pay income tax on growth when you take money out.
  • Basis: money you put in that was already taxed. It is not taxed again when taken out.
  • Index fund: one fund that holds many companies' stocks, like the S&P 500.

Frequently asked questions

Can a Trump Account invest in individual stocks or crypto?

No. During the growth period it can only hold eligible mutual funds or ETFs tracking a broad U.S. equity index, with no leverage and fees of 0.10% or less.

Do contributions to a Trump Account reduce my taxes?

No federal deduction is allowed for contributions before the year the child turns 18. Employer contributions up to $2,500 are excluded from the employee's income.

What happens at 18?

The account generally becomes subject to traditional IRA rules and belongs to the child.
