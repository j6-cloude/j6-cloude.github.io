What Is a Trump Account Invested In?

By j6 · Updated October 28, 2026 · Facts from IRS and Treasury sources (listed below) · Not tax advice

Short answer: right now all Trump Account money goes into one fund: SPYM, an S&P 500 index fund. It holds about 500 large U.S. companies. You cannot pick another fund yet. The official help center says more choices are expected later. The fund fee is 0.02% a year (about $2 per $10,000). There is no account fee.

The rules for what it can hold

  • Only mutual funds or ETFs that track a broad index of mostly U.S. stocks (like the S&P 500).
  • No leverage. No single stocks, bonds, cash or crypto.
  • Fund fees of 0.10% or less.
  • Money is invested within about 1 business day. Dividends are reinvested.

Is the money safe?

It is held in a trust account by a registered trustee. SIPC covers up to $500,000 if the broker fails. SIPC does not cover market losses. The account can drop in value, and the government does not guarantee it.

How much has $1,000 grown in the past?

So for a newborn, the middle result was about 6 times the money. Even in a bad stretch (10th percentile) it nearly tripled. Money added at 17 had only one year, and in a bad year it lost value.

What this means for you

  • Earlier money matters most. $1,000 at birth did far better than $1,000 at age 10.
  • You don't need to pick funds or time the market. There is one choice for now.
  • If your child will need the money right at 18, remember the last year can be a down year.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Trump Account invested in?

All contributions are automatically invested in SPYM, an S&P 500 index ETF. There is no other option yet; more choices are expected for claimed accounts later.

Can I choose investments in a Trump Account?

Not at this time. By law, options are limited to low-cost, unleveraged funds tracking a broad index of mainly U.S. stocks.

What are the fees on a Trump Account?

No fee to claim or maintain the account. Funds may charge an expense ratio of at most 0.1%; SPYM's is 0.02%.

Can a Trump Account lose money?

Yes. It is invested in stocks and not guaranteed by the government. SIPC protection does not cover market losses.

How much will $1,000 in a Trump Account be worth at 18?

Treasury's historical data (birth cohorts 1926–2006) shows a median of about $6,180 for $1,000 invested at birth, with a 10th–90th percentile range of $2,980 to $13,800.
