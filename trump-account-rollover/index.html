Trump Account Rollover Rules

By j6 · Updated October 28, 2026 · Facts from IRS and Treasury sources (listed below) · Not tax advice

Short answer: before 18, you can only move the whole account to a new Trump Account firm. Or, in the year your child turns 17, to an ABLE account. You can't move it to a 529 or a Roth IRA. You can't move a 529 into it, either. At 18 it is a normal IRA. Then your child can move it to any IRA firm. They can also switch it to a Roth IRA and pay tax on the taxable part.

Can you switch away from Robinhood?

Robinhood Securities is the first trustee. The rules allow a trustee-to-trustee move of the full balance to another approved Trump Account trustee. The official help center says this is available "when available," so for now there may be no other provider to move to. A move gives the account a new number; it does not reset any rules.

Roth conversion after 18: tax estimate

Converting moves money from the traditional IRA into a Roth IRA. Your child pays income tax now on the taxable part (seed, employer money, growth). After that, qualified Roth growth is tax-free. The family basis is not taxed again.

Frequently asked questions

Can a Trump Account be converted to a Roth IRA?

Not before 18. From the year the child turns 18 it follows traditional IRA rules, which include Roth conversions; the taxable part of the converted amount is taxed as ordinary income.

Can I roll a Trump Account into a 529?

No. The only rollovers allowed during the growth period are a full transfer to another Trump Account and an ABLE rollover in the year the child turns 17.

Can I roll a 529 or custodial IRA into a Trump Account?

No. The official help center says 529 plans and custodial IRAs cannot be rolled into a Trump Account, and ACATS transfers are not supported.

Can I move my child's Trump Account to Fidelity or Vanguard?

Only to another approved Trump Account trustee, and only the whole balance. After 18 it is a traditional IRA and can be moved to any IRA provider.

Can my child have two Trump Accounts?

Only one Trump Account with money in it at a time.
