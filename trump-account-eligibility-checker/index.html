Trump Account Eligibility Checker

Written by j6 · Updated October 27, 2026 · Based on IRS/Treasury guidance · Not tax advice

Three different things are often confused: (1) the account itself, (2) the $1,000 Treasury seed, and (3) the Dell $250 gift. Each has its own rules.

Eligibility rules at a glance

BenefitWho qualifiesHow to get it
Trump AccountAny child with a valid SSN who will not turn 18 before the end of the year the account is establishedTreasury auto-enrolled eligible children from Oct 1, 2026; claim it in the Trump Accounts app/website. Otherwise file Form 4547.
$1,000 Treasury seedU.S. citizens born Jan 1, 2025 – Dec 31, 2028 with a valid SSNMust be elected on Form 4547 (app, website, or IRS Online Account), even if the account was auto-opened
Dell $250Children born 2016–2024 in ZIP codes with median family income of about $117,300 or less, plus children on military basesAutomatic; no application. Check the ZIP on Invest America's lookup

Frequently asked questions

Is my child born in 2024 eligible for the $1,000?

No. The $1,000 seed is only for U.S. citizens born from January 1, 2025 through December 31, 2028. A 2024 child can still have a Trump Account and may get the Dell $250 if the ZIP code qualifies.

Do non-citizen children qualify?

A child with a valid SSN can have a Trump Account. The $1,000 seed requires U.S. citizenship.

Do I need to apply for the Dell $250?

No. According to Michael & Susan Dell's announcement, eligible children receive it automatically. The income threshold was set at a ZIP-code median family income of about $117,300 (as of Oct 9, 2026).

My child was auto-enrolled. Do I still need Form 4547?

Not to open the account, but yes to request the $1,000 seed if your child qualifies, and you must claim the account to manage it.
