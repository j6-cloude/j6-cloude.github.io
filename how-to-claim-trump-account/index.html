How to Claim a Trump Account

By j6 · Updated October 27, 2026 · Facts from IRS and Treasury sources (listed below) · Not tax advice

Short answer: since Oct 1, 2026, most kids under 18 with an SSN got an account opened for them. You claim it in the official Trump Accounts app or website. If your child was born 2025 to 2028, you also ask for the free $1,000 on Form 4547. It costs nothing.

Never pay to claim. Scammers copy this program. Start from trumpaccounts.gov or irs.gov/trumpaccounts. Don't give an SSN to anyone who texts or calls you first.

Step by step

  1. Get your details ready: your ID details, plus your child's legal name, SSN and date of birth, exactly as on their Social Security card.
  2. Open the official app or site: the Trump Accounts app (Apple App Store or Google Play), or the website linked from trumpaccounts.gov. Watch out for look-alike sites and texts. The SSA Inspector General warns about government imposter scams.
  3. Prove who you are and that you can manage the child's account.
  4. Claim and turn on the account.
  5. Ask for the $1,000 (U.S. citizen born 2025–2028): send Form 4547 and pick the pilot program contribution. You can do this in the app, on the website, or in your IRS Online Account.
  6. Optional: add money (up to $5,000 a year), and ask your employer if it adds money too.

Who runs it: Treasury picked BNY as financial agent. Robinhood Securities is the broker and first trustee (per the IRS FAQ). Help line: 866-USA-4547.

Got a rejection? See Form 4547 / CP318 fixes.

Frequently asked questions

Is Form 4547 required if my child was auto-enrolled?

Not to open the account. You still need Form 4547 to request the $1,000 seed, and you must claim the account to manage it.

Is Form 4547 a real IRS form?

Yes. The IRS confirms Form 4547, Trump Account Election(s), is an official form.

Who is the custodian of Trump Accounts?

The IRS FAQ says Treasury designated BNY as financial agent and Robinhood Securities as brokerage provider and initial trustee.
