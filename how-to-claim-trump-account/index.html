How to Claim a Trump Account

Written by j6 · Updated October 27, 2026 · Based on IRS/Treasury guidance · Not tax advice

Short answer: since October 1, 2026 the Treasury has auto-enrolled eligible children. Download the official Trump Accounts app (or use the website), verify your identity, claim the account, and separately elect the $1,000 seed on Form 4547 if your child was born 2025–2028.

Step-by-step

  1. Gather details: your name and identifying information, the child's legal name, Social Security number and date of birth, exactly as on Social Security records.
  2. Open the official channel: the Trump Accounts app (Apple App Store / Google Play) or the Trump Accounts website linked from trumpaccounts.gov. Beware of look-alike sites and texts; the SSA Inspector General warns of government-imposter scams.
  3. Verify your identity and your authority to manage the child's account.
  4. Claim and activate the account.
  5. Elect the $1,000 seed (born 2025–2028, U.S. citizen): submit Form 4547 and tick the pilot program contribution, via the app, the website, or your IRS Online Account.
  6. Optional: set up contributions (up to $5,000/yr) and ask your employer whether it offers Trump Account contributions.

Who runs it: Treasury designated BNY as financial agent; Robinhood Securities is the brokerage provider and initial trustee (per the IRS FAQ). Help line: 866-USA-4547.

Got a rejection? See Form 4547 / CP318 fixes.

Frequently asked questions

Is Form 4547 required if my child was auto-enrolled?

Not to open the account. You still need Form 4547 to request the $1,000 seed, and you must claim the account to manage it.

Is Form 4547 a real IRS form?

Yes. The IRS confirms Form 4547, Trump Account Election(s), is an official form.

Who is the custodian of Trump Accounts?

The IRS FAQ says Treasury designated BNY as financial agent and Robinhood Securities as brokerage provider and initial trustee.
