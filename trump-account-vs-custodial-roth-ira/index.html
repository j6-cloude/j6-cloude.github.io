Trump Account vs Custodial Roth IRA vs UTMA

By j6 · Updated October 27, 2026 · Facts from IRS and Treasury sources (listed below) · Not tax advice

Short answer: a custodial Roth IRA is the best tax deal (tax-free growth) but needs the child to have earned income. A Trump Account needs no earned income but growth is taxed on withdrawal. A UTMA/UGMA has no limits or investment restrictions but its earnings are taxed yearly (kiddie tax) and the child controls it at 18–21.

Frequently asked questions

Is a Roth IRA for kids better than a Trump Account?

If your child has earned income, a custodial Roth IRA usually wins on taxes because qualified growth is tax-free. Many families can use both: the Trump Account for the free seed and non-earned money, the Roth for earned income.

Does a Trump Account count against FAFSA?

Federal guidance on how Trump Accounts are treated for financial aid is still developing; check studentaid.gov before relying on any assumption.
