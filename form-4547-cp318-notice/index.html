Form 4547 Rejected or Notice CP318: How to Fix It

By j6 · Updated October 27, 2026 · Facts from IRS and Treasury sources (listed below) · Not tax advice

Short answer: Notice CP318 means the IRS could not complete or process your Trump Account election, most often because a name, SSN or date of birth does not match Social Security records. Fix the data and resubmit Form 4547 as the notice instructs. Do not file Form 1040-X.

Common causes and fixes

CauseWhat to check / do
Child's name, SSN or DOB mismatchCompare with the Social Security card/record exactly (suffixes, hyphens, recent name changes). Resubmit Form 4547 per the notice.
Your (responsible party's) details don't match IRS recordsUse the same legal name, SSN/ITIN and DOB as on your tax return.
Account or election already existsAnother parent or guardian may already have elected; a child can have one funded Trump Account at a time. Claim the existing account in the app.
Missing or unreadable informationCommon with forms filed alongside a tax return; resubmit electronically via the app, website or IRS Online Account.
Child outside the age windowThe account must be established before the year the child turns 18.

What not to do

  • Don't file an amended return (1040-X) to fix Form 4547 — the IRS says not to.
  • Don't expect IRS phone agents to correct the election; the IRS says they cannot. For account changes after claiming, the program line is 866-USA-4547.
  • Don't give SSNs to anyone who contacts you first by text or phone.

Frequently asked questions

Why did I get Notice CP318?

The IRS needs more action to complete or correct a Trump Account election, typically due to missing or mismatched information.

Should I file Form 1040-X to fix Form 4547?

No. The IRS says not to; follow the CP318 instructions and resubmit Form 4547.

Can the IRS fix my election by phone?

The IRS says its customer service representatives cannot correct the election by phone or help claim the account.
