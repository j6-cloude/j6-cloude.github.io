Trump Account Deadlines and Key Dates

By j6 · Updated October 28, 2026 · Facts from IRS and Treasury sources (listed below) · Not tax advice

Short answer: there is no deadline to claim your child's account. The real cut-offs are about age: the account must be set up before the year your child turns 18, and deposits stop after Dec 31 of the year they turn 17. The $1,000 is for babies born from Jan 1, 2025 to Dec 31, 2028. You can elect it any time.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a deadline to sign up for a Trump Account?

No deadline to claim an account that was opened for your child. An account must be set up before the year the child turns 18.

Is there a deadline for the $1,000 Trump Account money?

No fixed filing deadline was announced; the official help center says you can elect it any time. The child must be a U.S. citizen born January 1, 2025 through December 31, 2028.

What was the June 30 Trump Account date?

June 30, 2026 is when the IRS let people file Form 4547 through their IRS online account and announced gift-tax relief. It was not a sign-up deadline.

When do Trump Account contributions stop?

After December 31 of the year the child turns 17.

When did automatic enrollment start?

Treasury began opening accounts automatically for eligible children on October 1, 2026. Parents still need to claim them.
