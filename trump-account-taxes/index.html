Trump Account Taxes: What Is Taxed and When

By j6 · Updated October 28, 2026 · Facts from IRS and Treasury sources (listed below) · Not tax advice

Short answer: a Trump Account is tax-deferred, not tax-free. You get no tax deduction for what you put in. No one pays tax on the growth each year. When money comes out (age 18 or later), the growth is taxed as your child's income. The money family put in is not taxed again.

Each kind of money, in one line
  • Family money: no deduction going in; tax-free coming out.
  • The $1,000 seed and charity gifts: taxed as income coming out.
  • Employer money: not taxed to you going in (up to $2,500); taxed coming out.
  • Growth: not taxed each year; taxed coming out.

Tax forms

  • Form 5498-TA: sent each year for each account that got contributions. It is for your records; you generally do not file it.
  • Form 1099-R: only if money comes out (for example, an excess contribution is returned).
  • No 1099-DIV or 1099-INT. Dividends inside the account are not taxed each year, so there is no kiddie tax on them.
  • Form 4547: the election form to open an account or get the $1,000. It is not a tax bill.

Other tax points

  • You cannot count a deposit made in early 2027 toward 2026. Every deposit counts for the calendar year it is made.
  • Gifts from relatives can need a gift tax return only in rare cases. See grandparents and gift tax.
  • Keep records of what family put in. That is the basis your child will not pay tax on later.

Frequently asked questions

Are Trump Account contributions tax deductible?

No. Personal contributions from parents, relatives or friends are not deductible. Employer contributions up to $2,500 per employee are excluded from the employee's income instead.

Is a Trump Account tax-free?

No. It is tax-deferred. Earnings are not taxed each year, but earnings, the $1,000 seed, employer money and charity gifts are taxed as ordinary income when withdrawn. Family contributions (basis) come out tax-free.

What tax form do I get for a Trump Account?

Form 5498-TA each year the account gets contributions, and Form 1099-R only if a permitted distribution is made. You generally do not need to file Form 5498-TA with your return.

Do I have to report Trump Account earnings on my child's tax return each year?

No. Earnings stay tax-deferred inside the account, and the account does not issue 1099-DIV or 1099-INT.

Can I make a Trump Account contribution for last year?

No. Contributions count toward the limit for the calendar year in which they are made.
