How Do I Know If My Child Has a Trump Account?

By j6 · Updated October 27, 2026 · Facts from IRS and Treasury sources (listed below) · Not tax advice

Short answer: if your child has a valid SSN and is under 18, the Treasury has probably opened one automatically (auto-enrollment began October 1, 2026). To confirm, use the official Trump Accounts app or website: the IRS says you can check there whether an account has been established and then claim it. You may also get a Treasury letter if your child may qualify for the $1,000 contribution.

How to check, step by step

  1. Install the Trump Accounts app from the Apple App Store or Google Play, or go to the website linked from trumpaccounts.gov.
  2. Verify your identity and your authority over the child.
  3. Enter the child's legal name, SSN and date of birth exactly as on Social Security records. If an account exists, you will be able to claim and activate it.
  4. Born 2025–2028? Separately elect the $1,000 seed (Form 4547) — auto-enrollment does not elect it for you.
  5. Born 2016–2024? Check whether the Dell $250 was deposited: it went automatically to kids in ZIP codes with median family income ≤ $117,300 or on military bases, all by Oct 9, 2026 (see the eligibility checker). (Source: Invest America, figures as of Oct 9, 2026; verified Oct 27, 2026.)

Is the Trump Account app legit?

The IRS's own Trump Accounts page tells parents to download the Trump Accounts app from the Apple App Store or Google Play to claim an account, and its FAQ names BNY as Treasury's financial agent and Robinhood Securities as brokerage provider and initial trustee. So the official app is real — but scammers imitate it. Safe habits:

  • Start from irs.gov/trumpaccounts or trumpaccounts.gov, not from a text, email or ad link.
  • No one from the program needs you to pay a fee to receive the $1,000 or $250.
  • Don't give an SSN to anyone who contacts you first. The official help line is 866-USA-4547 (866-872-4547).

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if my child has a Trump Account?

Use the official Trump Accounts app or website; the IRS says it lets you check whether an account was established for your child and begin claiming it. Since October 1, 2026 eligible children have been auto-enrolled.

Is the Trump Account app legit?

Yes, the IRS directs parents to the Trump Accounts app on the Apple App Store and Google Play. Reach it from irs.gov/trumpaccounts or trumpaccounts.gov to avoid imitations.

Does auto-enrollment include the $1,000?

No. You must still elect the $1,000 pilot contribution on Form 4547 if your child qualifies.

Do I have to put money in?

No. Contributions are optional; the account can hold just the seed or Dell gift.
